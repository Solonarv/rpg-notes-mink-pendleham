Yala and Artur set to deciphering the manifest, which takes them about four hours.

They don't get everything, and there appears to be a bunch of information missing.

About a third of the stuff is decipherable.
- mechanical components
- biomechanical components
- industrial equipment/supplies
- various beasts
	- the bear is a "red bear"
	- confirmation that the one barrel which appeared to contain blood wasps did indeed contain those (alongside a note not to open it.)

approximate value: 10k gold for the whole ship, incl. ~1.5k for the machine.

We spend a long time debating how exactly to ensure we get our share, how the harbormaster might screw us on the deal, and what we can do about it.

We eventually realize he probably doesn't expect a full report from us anyway. If we did give one, it would be very strange; he'd either suspect we were keeping something from him, or take us for suckers.

If we just take the machine and he found out, this would not be some unconscionable violation that he would attempt to kill us over, he just might try to bully us into taking it out of our share or whatever.

There is a reason he didn't loot the ship yet; most of his people just aren't that strong and he doesn't want to just throw their lives away, especially being very uncertain about our actual power.

Knowing this, we stash the face machine (most likely, not like we can be sure without assembling it) in our extradimensional lab, and Artur sweeps some dust and such over the location and tracks to perhaps hide that we took it.

Yala changes her prepared spells out of an abundance of caution.

We realize we didn't enter the captain's quarters, and decide they are a tomorrow problem.

Yala *prestidigitates* the hammocks clean, and we bed down for the night. Artur's exposure catches up to him, leaving him sickened 2 and coughing up blood. However, nothing interrupts our sleep.

In the morning, Yala conjures up some bland food (except not, because she has *least wish* - I mean, *prestidigitation*) to get us through the day, and Artur drops some life boosts on us to recover some of our no-longer-drained health. He then rests for 30 minutes to regain his focus points; we are still not interrupted.

We decide to go poke the captain's quarters now. Fabian bravely begins trying the doors.

Doors 1 and 4 (on the left and right) aren't locked, and lead to some rooms - officer's/passenger's quarters, now ransacked. Doors 2 and 3 are locked.

Artur immediately unlocks the first door he attacks (32, with a nat 20). We open the door, and inside is a crazed man waving a gun around. He yells imprecations and shoots in our direction. Then he drops his gun (it's single-shot) and draws another

Yala's entreaties don't work, he shoots again and pulls out a THIRD gun from... somewhere.

She says will you STOP trying to shoot us!! we are trying to have a conversation.

Yala explains about our deal with the bandits, which we made not knowing there would be anyone alive on the ship. She adds that we're entirely willing to leave him alone, but the bandits might not be.

He says "so leave!", which we aren't willing to do. He closes the door in our face and begins barricading it; we make no attempt to stop him.

We decide to leave him to stew in there, waiting on the deck for someone to show up.

A few hours later, an enforcer shows up, and Yala informs him of what happened.

Yala then goes to tell the captain that the harbormaster's people are coming to kill him, and if he wants to get out, he has but to say the word. His response is to shoot her, clipping her ear for 4 damage. She yells "don't say I didn't warn you!" and leaves, easily avoiding the obvious repartee shot.

Quurak asks how we were planning to carry our share, and seeing our lack of obvious answers, he offers to just buy it out. He offers 2000 gp.

Yala counteroffers 3000 gp, referencing the 85k total value and the 60-40 split that we agreed on, which would have us get 3400 gp.

With Artur's help, this succeeds magnificiently, and we get 3000 gp.
