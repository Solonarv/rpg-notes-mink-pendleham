Having searched the bodies (and this deck), we find a logbook, which was in the bear's lair, on (presumably) the captain's body. It records most of what the ship was transporting. No keys, though, except for the cabins we already opened.

We head down to the next deck...

There are some stacks of barrels here. wooden, of good make, not leaking. Nothing suspicious around them. Some kind of stamp on the lid, a number. Some of the barrels have been rolled away and opened in the middle of the room.

Yala checks on the open barrels.
- contains some mechanical components, some kind of engine or pump.
- red wax on the sides, center empty, probably contained the bees: this is what a bee nest looks like when they're not infesting people. clearly they've gone somewhere else.
- completely empty, smells meaty, was well sealed
- some kind of sand/dust, someone dug through it.

We see more stairs heading further down. Many hammocks along the sides of the room. Many barrels and crates everywhere.

There are some doors nearby, previously broken into. 
- treasury, already partly ransacked: the treasury seems to be missing the money chest, books scattered on the ground.
- cabin where someone must have slept

Toward the front, we find a few tables with chairs, and something like a bar/kitchen opposite them.

As we approach the ladder leading down into the hold, we notice it's blocked by a grate with heavy things stacked on top.

There are three more doors by the front:
- a doctor's office, already ransacked; seems like the culprit was looking for something specific
- carpentry workshop with tools, wood, nails, etc.
- more food storage, big barrel half full of water.

Inside the prow, there are some crates converted to tables with tablecloths, where sailors played cards.

Yala pries loose one of the boards covering up the cargo hold and peeks into the darkness.
- cargo space
- pretty clean in this central space
- hard to see in the dark, everything down there seems to be shimmering - as if someone spilled half a ton of glitter. herbal smell.
- no (visible) enemies.

The smell begins to spread, and Fabian identifies it as some kind of fungal spores. Probably not good to breathe.

After some deliberation, Yala fashions some damp-cloth masks to protect from the spores, then she un-boards the grate over the ladder. Artur easily picks the padlock holding the grate shut, and Yala looks down. She sees a small wooden landing at the bottom of the stairs, and little else. It's full of spores.

We decide to go down, holding a lungful of air as we descend.

There is a small landing, and two corridors leading to the back of the ship. Yala (the only one who can see) leads the others down one of the corridors (the left one). We go all the way to the back of the ship without spotting anything, then Yala looks up the right-hand corridor and spots an ambulatory mushroom creature shedding spores, walking toward us.

Round 1:
- Yala casts *cleanse air*, creating a clean space on top of us. She Points Out the mushroom.
- Fabian lights his lantern now that there's no risk of a spore dust explosion.
- Artur *evil eyes* the basidrond, then cast *revealing light* on it. It's dazzled for two rounds.
- The basidrond serenely shtomps over to fabian and attempts to eat(?) him. First attack misses, second hits for 21 bludg. He succeeds the mystery fort save.
Round 2:
- Yala screams "do mushrooms bleed??" and delays
- Fabian flurries the mushroom. It's very soft and spongy; he cuts and claws away half of the mushroom's body.
- Artur recalls his lore: Mushrooms *do* bleed. He throws some *needle darts*, 26 hits for 15 p.
- Yala casts *summon elemental* to summon a ferrous butterfly. Its first melee attack already causes the mushroom to fall apart into pieces.

This was the last enemy on the ship (except one, which "doesn't count"). With the logbook in hand, we can find the crate with our target.
